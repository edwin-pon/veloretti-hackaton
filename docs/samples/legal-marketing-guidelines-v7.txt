VELORETTI
Legal guidelines for marketing, version 7

Demo stand-in. No legal source was supplied for this project. Every rule,
section number and citation below is written for the demo and carries no legal
weight whatsoever.

1  SCOPE

These guidelines apply to every asset that leaves the studio, in all four
served markets, paid and owned alike.

2  CLAIMS

2.1  Comparative claims

"Never name a competitor. Ever."

Category comparisons are permitted where a third-party source is reproduced in
the same asset.

2.2  Superlatives

"Superlatives require measurement."

No absolute superlative (best, fastest, safest, longest range) may appear
unless a footnoted measurement accompanies it in the same asset.

2.3  Performance figures

Any figure describing range, speed or load must match the approved product
claim list held by product marketing.

2.4  Sources

A reproduced source carries the name of the testing body and the date of the
test. A source without a date is not a source.

3  SUSTAINABILITY WORDING

"Climate neutral", "CO2 neutral" and "green" may not be used without a linked
calculation method, per the EU Green Claims Directive.

"Lower emissions than a car trip" is approved with the standard footnote.

"See memo GC-2025-04 for permitted phrasings."

The memo is not reproduced here. Two approved examples appear in the annex.

4  DISCLAIMERS

4.1  Placement

A disclaimer sits in the asset that carries the claim. A disclaimer on a
landing page does not cover an ad.

4.2  Range claims

"Use the approved footnote unchanged."

  Range measured per WLTP-e cycle at 25 km/h assist, 75 kg rider, level
  terrain. Real-world range varies.

The closing sentence is required in Germany and is therefore included by
default everywhere.

5  DATA AND CONSENT

5.1  Basis

Opt-in across all four markets. Double opt-in in Germany.

"Soft opt-in is not used by Veloretti."

5.2  Profiling

Audience segments may not be built on inferred health or income data.

5.3  Children

No audience may be targeted below 18. Kids-line campaigns address parents.

5.4  Retention

"24 months from last engagement."

The trigger is last engagement, not the date of collection.

6  MARKET-SPECIFIC

6.1  Germany, UWG

"Refer to Kanzlei opinion of 12 March."

No rule text is set out in this section. Two examples appear in the footnotes:
price advertising states whether VAT and assembly are included, and dealer
availability claims carry the qualifier "at participating dealers".

6.2  Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark

No market-specific additions at version 7.

7  APPROVAL

"Approval as agreed per campaign."

No owner and no threshold are named. The examples in this section are all paid
media.

ANNEX  APPROVED SUSTAINABILITY PHRASINGS

  Lower emissions than the same trip by car.
  Replaces a car trip, not a bus route.
