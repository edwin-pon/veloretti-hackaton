04  PALETTE

PRIMARY

  #1A1A1A   Ink black          Pantone Black 6 C

"Monochrome first. The product is the colour."

The bike carries the colour. Everything around it is ink, paper and grey. Ink
black is the only swatch on this page marked primary, and it is the only one
that may set type at any size.

TERTIARY

  #FE5900   Signal orange

"Tertiary is not fixed. Special cases only."

Signal orange is promotional. Discounts, sale, urgency. It is not a brand
colour and not a default call to action, and the guide states it is not fixed:
a future promotion may replace it.

06  USING COLOUR

"Reserved for discounts and promotional messages."

Signal orange marks a promotion and nothing else. Never as a background behind
body copy. Never on type below 18px.

"Sentence case everywhere except product names."

Headlines, subheads and subject lines are sentence case. Forty examples follow
in this guide and none of them deviate.

08  PUNCTUATION

"No serial comma."

British-English house style. Lists read: the carrier, the basket and the child
seat.

"We do not shout."

Exclamation marks are forbidden. No stated exceptions.
<<<PAGE>>>
09  TYPEFACE

"One typeface does everything."

PP Neue Montreal, Regular 400 and Medium 500. Those two weights were supplied
as web fonts and no other weight appears in this specimen.

"Display is large and tight."

Display and headings are set Medium at -2% tracking. The licence note confirms
web use is covered.
